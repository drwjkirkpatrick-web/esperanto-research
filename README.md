## What this is

A grounded, citation-verified review of **OPUS23 | ESPERANTO** — Datapunk Bioinformatics' multi-omics clinical decision-support platform (Dr. Peter D'Adamo's successor to Opus23 Pro) — built around a seed document:

**The gap analysis** (`docs/gap-analysis-30-modules.md`) proposes 30 candidate additions (styled "modules" in the source document) derivable from ESPERANTO's existing vault (4,500+ curated SNPs, 22,800 microbial taxa, 3,200+ metabolites). It was generated by a Hermes agent paired with Kimi 2.6 attached to Firecrawl through Nous.

The analysis is preserved verbatim with a provenance header. Every claim it makes is checked — against public platform documentation, the eight named competitors' own pages, and PubMed-indexed medical literature — in `research/`.

## The platform in one breath

COGENT reads the genome (chips or whole-genome VCFs). FLORIAN reads the gut (22,800 taxa behind every stool report). KINESIS reads the biochemistry (390 explained markers against 3,200+ metabolites, 81 ingestible report formats). One client record spans all three, and a dedicated language model writes every case summary, answer, and next-step plan with a clickable citation behind each claim.

## The 30 proposed additions

From the gap analysis (styled "modules" in the source document). Data flow and effort estimates are the analysis's own; audit findings from our dossiers are appended where they bear.

1. **Pharmacogenomic Dosing Calculator (PGx Core)** — FDA/CPIC-guided dosing from CYP2D6, CYP2C19, CYP3A4/5, CYP2C9, TPMT, DPYD, SLCO1B1, VKORC1 with drug-by-drug adjustments. Data: COGENT SNP panel + drug–gene interactions + ClinVar. Effort: low. *Audit: the COGENT drug–gene + ClinVar premise is publicly documented; insider review reports this is already built (research/04 §1).*
2. **Methylation Capacity Index (MCI)** — composite "methylation flux" from MTHFR (C677T, A1298C), MTR, MTRR, BHMT, PEMT, COMT, MAT1A, SHMT1, with bottleneck readouts. COGENT + KINESIS. Effort: low. *Audit: no validated composite methylation-capacity score exists in the literature; ACMG states MTHFR testing has minimal clinical utility (research/05 §5).*
3. **Detoxification Burden Score (DBS)** — phase I (CYP1A2, CYP2E1) vs phase II (GSTT1, GSTM1, GSTP1, SULT1A1, NAT2, UGT1A1) capacity measured against organic-acid/toxic-metabolite load. COGENT ↔ KINESIS. Effort: low-medium.
4. **Mitochondrial Haplogroup Stress Index (MHSI)** — mtDNA haplogroup from WGS VCF, cross-referenced with Mitome cheek-swab data when present. COGENT + KINESIS. Effort: medium. *Audit: genotyping chips carry sparse mtDNA markers, but this addition's stated input is WGS VCF — a documented ingest format — so the caveat applies only to array-sourced clients (research/05 §3).*
5. **ABO-Secretor-Disease Risk Matrix (ASDRM)** — conditional risk profiles from ABO, FUT2 (secretor) and Lewis status (e.g. non-secretor × Crohn's, O × norovirus resistance), with food/supplement suggestions. COGENT + FLORIAN. Effort: low. *Audit: COGENT publicly documents ABO, secretor (FUT2) and Lewis status; insider review reports this exists as the platform's own core logic (research/04 §1).*
6. **Histamine Intolerance Score (HIS)** — DAO (AOC1), HNMT, MAOA, MAOB, ALDH2 vs gut histamine producers/degraders — a load-vs-clearance ratio. All three instruments. Effort: medium.
7. **Oxalate Metabolism Risk Score (OMRS)** — AGXT, GRHPR, HOGA1, SLC26A1 vs Oxalobacter formigenes and other oxalate-degrading taxa; flags calcium-oxalate stone risk. Effort: low.
8. **Lactose Persistence ↔ Gut Lactobacillus Correlator (LP-GLC)** — LCT -13910 C/T against Lactobacillus/Bifidobacteria abundance. COGENT ↔ FLORIAN. Effort: low.
9. **Gluten Sensitivity Polygenic Panel (GSPP)** — beyond HLA-DQ2/DQ8: MYO9B, IL21, cross-referenced with zonulin, calprotectin, anti-gliadin markers. Effort: medium. *Audit: HLA-DQ2/DQ8 inference needs six tag SNPs and is clinically a rule-out test per ESPGHAN (research/05 §2).*
10. **Sulfur Metabolism Index (SMI)** — CBS, SUOX, BHMT, CTH vs sulfate-reducing Desulfovibrio and urinary sulfate/sulfite. All three instruments. Effort: medium.
11. **Vitamin D Metabolism Score (VDMS)** — CYP2R1, GC (VDBP), VDR (FokI, BsmI, ApaI, TaqI), CYP24A1 plus 25-OH-D and 1,25-OH-D: conversion, transport, receptor sensitivity, degradation. Effort: low. *Audit: VDR variant effects are trivially small, and VDR is absent from the major 25(OH)D GWAS loci — a VDR-weighted score would overstate it (research/05 §4).*
12. **Iron Metabolism Index (IMI)** — HFE (C282Y, H63D), TMPRSS6, TFR2, SLC40A1 vs ferritin, TIBC, serum iron, transferrin saturation. Effort: low.
13. **Cholesterol Synthesis vs Absorption Balance (CSAB)** — HMGCR (synthesis) vs NPC1L1 (absorption) + APOE, cross-checked with lipid panels; statin-vs-ezetimibe signal. Effort: low-medium.
14. **Estrogen Metabolism Safety Index (EMSI)** — CYP1B1/CYP1A1 plus COMT, SULT1A1, GSTP1, UGT1A1 against DUTCH 4-OH:2-OH:16α-OH pathway ratios. Effort: medium.
15. **Cortisol Clearance Genotype-Phenotype Mismatch (CCGM)** — CYP3A4/5, HSD11B1, HSD11B2 vs DUTCH/ASI cortisol curves; explains total-vs-free discordance. Effort: medium.
16. **Thyroid Conversion Efficiency (TCE)** — DIO1, DIO2 (Thr92Ala), DIO3, THRB vs TSH, fT4, fT3, rT3 — conversion vs primary vs receptor-resistance patterns. Effort: low.
17. **Neurotransmitter Synthesis Capacity (NSC)** — TPH2, DDC, MAOA, MAOB, COMT, DBH, GAD1/2 plus organic acids (5-HIAA, HVA, VMA). Effort: medium.
18. **One-Carbon Flux Model (OCFM)** — systems model of the methionine–folate–B12 cycle (MTHFR, MTR, MTRR, BHMT, RFC1, TCN2, CBS, MUT) fed by homocysteine, FIGLU, MMA; outputs the bottleneck. Effort: high.
19. **Omega-3 Responsive Index Prediction (ORIP)** — FADS1 (rs174537), FADS2, ELOVL2 predicting Omega-3 Index response to EPA/DHA, validated against measured values. Effort: low.
20. **Gut-Brain Axis Risk Score (GBARS)** — taxa producing GABA, serotonin precursors, butyrate or LPS × neurotransmitter SNPs; flags genetic-vulnerability + dysbiosis double hits. COGENT + FLORIAN. Effort: medium.
21. **TMAO Cardiovascular Risk Integrator (TCRI)** — FMO3 × TMA-producing bacteria (Proteus, Providencia, Desulfovibrio) × choline metabolites. All three instruments. Effort: medium.
22. **Urea Cycle Capacity Profiler (UCCP)** — CPS1, ASS1, ASL, ARG1, OTC vs ammonia, amino-acid panel and organic acids. Effort: medium.
23. **Glycemic Response Prediction (GRP)** — TCF7L2, SLC30A8, PPARG, KCNJ11, FTO vs fasting insulin/glucose, HbA1c, HOMA-IR. Effort: low-medium.
24. **Saturated Fat Sensitivity Index (SFSI)** — APOA2 (rs5082) + PPARG predicting LDL response to saturated fat, checked against lipid panels. Effort: low. *Audit: the original interaction was replicated in three cohorts, but post-2019 evidence is judged insufficient for genotype-guided prescribing (research/05 §6).*
25. **Caffeine Metabolism Index (CMI)** — CYP1A2 half-life × cortisol awakening response (DUTCH/ASI) and sleep markers; flags slow metabolizers with HPA dysregulation. Effort: low.
26. **Alcohol Metabolism Risk Profile (AMRP)** — ADH1B, ADH1C, ALDH2 acetaldehyde exposure × GGT, ALT, AST, MCV. Effort: low.
27. **Salicylate / Phase II Sensitivity Score (SPII)** — NAT2, UGT1A1, SULT1A1 predicting salicylate/benzoate/phenolic sensitivity, cross-referenced with food-antibody panels and organic acids. Effort: low.
28. **Gut Mucosal Integrity Index (GMII)** — tight-junction SNPs (CDH1, OCLN, TJP1) × mucosal taxa × zonulin, calprotectin, IgA. All three instruments. Effort: medium. *Audit: the source document's taxa attribution is inverted — zonulin is a host protein (pre-haptoglobin-2), not produced by the named bacteria; and these SNPs show no validated permeability prediction (research/05 §1, §7).*
29. **Bone Remodeling Balance (BRB)** — VDR (BsmI, FokI), COL1A1 (Sp1), ESR1, GC × bone-turnover markers, calcium, PTH, vitamin D. Effort: low-medium. *Audit: same VDR caveat as addition 11 (research/05 §4).*
30. **Inflammatory Burden Polygenic Score (IBPS)** — IL6 (rs1800795), TNF (rs1800629), IL1B, CRP (rs1205), NFKB1, modulated by endotoxin-producers and hs-CRP. All three instruments. Effort: medium.

The analysis also proposes a three-phase rollout: quick wins (1, 2, 5, 8, 11, 12, 16, 19, 25, 26) → cross-instrument combinations (3, 6, 7, 9, 10, 13, 14, 15, 17, 21, 24, 27) → systems modeling (4, 18, 20, 22, 23, 28, 29, 30). Our synthesis's counter-reading: these land best as a *small* set of engines over guideline-grade genotype–phenotype relationships presented as ranges and evidence chains, not thirty standalone scores (`docs/synthesis.md`).

## Method

Every sourced claim carries a `[n]` citation; every cited source carries at least one **verbatim quote**, machine-checked against the saved page text in `extracts/`. Citation integrity is enforced with `sources.py verify --evidence` (grounded-citations skill). Insider claims that no public page can confirm stay attributed insider statements — never promoted to fact.

Scope: public documentation and medical literature only. This is a research document, not clinical advice, and not affiliated with Datapunk.
