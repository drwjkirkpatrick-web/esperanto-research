How To Get Started

* * *

Qualified practitioners take [a day-long training webinar.](https://datapunk.net/opus23blog/2021/01/28/certification-training-update/) We've coded a special ‘training veneer’ into the Opus23 program that allows students to work directly inside of Opus23, developing a curation strategy on a shared single client who has a complex health history. Each student’s curation selections are then saved to a unique data file linked to their account. This information is monitored by Dr. D’Adamo to assess competency by comparing student curation selections to similar curations performed by a panel of seasoned Opus23 professional users. When the student’s curation levels meet the minimum accuracy of the aggregated panel curations, the student essentially ‘passes’ certification. The new learning platform also features several new screencast videos of Dr. D’Adamo working through basic usage and curation. Students can work along with the videos for a true interactive experiences.

After successful completion of the basic training webinar new clients are added by securing upload licenses. Each license is for one client and allows you unlimited acces to the client data and all Opus23 functions.
