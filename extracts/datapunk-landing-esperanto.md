A dedicated large language model, in the background

## Read everything, cogitate relentlessly, show work.

Our generative large language model is extraordinary at connecting evidence and, using a proprietary nurturing schema, serves it to the clinician as high-value suggestions for the client's next steps. OPUS23 \| ESPERANTO keeps it in the background: it gives the model the patient's actual data and nothing else, and holds it to account for every sentence.

Brief

#### The case, summarized

Three or four screens that a busy practitioner reads first: the themes that stand out, the genetic reasons behind them, the microbiome's part, and what to consider — written in the tone you choose, from skeptical to embracing of the evidence.

Ask

#### A conversation about this patient

"Why is her FIGLU high?" "Which of his variants bear on methylation?" The model looks things up in the patient's own genome, stool and lab data as it answers — and says so plainly when the data don't cover a point.

Citations

#### Every claim, clickable

Genes, SNPs, markers, taxa, metabolites and agents are tagged in the text. Click one and the evidence opens: the genotype, the value against its range, the metabolite's page.

Opie

#### New research, matched to each patient, every night

Opie is our literature agent. Every night it reads PubMed and ClinVar for what each of your patients has: their diagnoses, the variants they carry and the genes behind them, the gut microbes out of range on their last stool test, their flagged lab results and the supplements they take. The language model keeps only human evidence that bears on care, names the agent that was tested, and writes one plain line with the reference. Then each paper is matched back to the whole patient: a new trial of a botanical for eczema that also names a gene your eczema patient carries goes to the top of that patient's page, and into the Brief and Ask. When ClinVar changes the classification of a variant your patient carries, you see that too. And you can choose to be alerted by email when Opie finds something significant for a patient: a ClinVar reclassification, a trial on their diagnosis, a treatment that helped their condition, or a paper that names two or more of their findings. Alerts are switched on for all your patients or one at a time, and the email carries no names or health details, only a link to sign in.

**De-identified** The client's name and ID are removed before anything is sent, and the request is checked on the way out.

**Grounded** The model may only use the case file it is given. It is told to name food and supplement explanations before a pathological reading.

**Your judgment** Decision support for licensed practitioners — never a diagnosis, never a prescription.

## Frequently asked questions

What is OPUS23 \| ESPERANTO?

OPUS23 \| ESPERANTO is the new generation of Opus23, the genomic exploration platform practitioners have relied on for years. It keeps everything Opus23 did well — the curated SNP interpretations, the multi-SNP algorithms, the drug–gene work — and adds two new instruments: FLORIAN for the gut microbiome and KINESIS for metabolomics, all sharing one client record and one reasoning layer.

Who is it for?

Licensed health professionals — physicians, naturopathic doctors, nutritionists, chiropractors and other practitioners who work with genetic, microbiome and functional lab data. It is decision support for the professional, not a consumer product.

Does my patient need a whole genome?

No. A consumer chip from 23andMe, AncestryDNA or MyHeritage gives a rich picture across more than 4,500 curated SNPs. A whole-genome VCF adds every other variant, ClinVar screening and full coverage — and can be added later, on top of an existing chip, without losing anything.

Which lab reports can KINESIS and FLORIAN read?

Mosaic OAT, MOAT and Omega-3 Index; DUTCH; Genova NutrEval, Metabolomix+, Organix, Cardio Check and food antibodies; Cyrex Arrays 2, 3X, 4, 5, 8, 10, 11, 12 and 20; the Diagnostic Solutions OAp organic acids profile; Genova Thyroid Plus; Doctor’s Data RBC elements; the Precision Point P88 dietary antigen test; ZRT, DiagnosTechs and Vibrant panels; the Mitome mitochondrial analysis; Viome; GI-MAP; Quest and Labcorp; and Epic MyChart and Cerner patient-portal results are read exactly, line by line. Thryve, BiomeSight and uBiome 16S files are read directly. Other labs’ PDFs are read with model-assisted extraction, with the patient’s details removed first and every value verified against the report. The full list is under “What you can upload”.

Is the language model making the clinical decisions?

No. The Brief, Ask and Next Steps are written from the patient's own data and nothing else, every claim is cited so you can check it in one click, and the model is instructed to rule out food, supplement and medication explanations before a pathological reading. The clinical judgment — and the decisions — remain yours.

What is sent to the language model? Is my patient's privacy protected?

The model receives a de-identified case file: results, references and the relevant genes and organisms — never the patient's name or ID, and every outgoing request is checked for them. Genome files stay on our own server, are never sold and never shared.

How is it priced?

Pay as you go, with tokens. Adding a genome, a microbiome sample or a lab panel uses tokens, as do the Brief, Ask and Next Steps; browsing, the Case File and the reference libraries do not. Creating a client record is free. There is no subscription to forget about.

I used the original Opus23. What happens to my clients?

They come with you. Accounts from the original Opus23 can be linked, and clients imported with their genome data intact, so their history carries straight into COGENT.

Do I need to install anything?

Nothing. OPUS23 \| ESPERANTO runs in any modern browser on desktop, laptop or tablet, and every improvement reaches you the moment it ships.

Is OPUS23 \| ESPERANTO a diagnostic test?

No. OPUS23 \| ESPERANTO interprets data your patients have already obtained, for education and clinical decision support. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe, and it is no substitute for a physician's evaluation.
