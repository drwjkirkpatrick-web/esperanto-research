## Your Tuesday, with the work done — not just recorded.

Six moments you will recognize, in clock order. In each one an agent does the work and you approve what reaches the chart, the outbox and the claim. The EHR keeps the record; you keep the last word.

1. 7:40 AM
Your MA is at the printer. The first patient is in the parking lot, **finishing intake on a phone-verified link**, consent signed, before the front door is unlocked.
no clipboard, nothing retyped
2. 9:15 AM
You look at the patient, not the screen. **Say it once and it is in the note**, in your template, every value tied to the second of audio it came from. Done when the patient stands up.
say it once
3. 12:10 PM

the level you earned, coded

Notes signed, the call booked, the fax answered, the level approved. Wherever a chart, an outbox or a claim was touched, **a person had the last word**. The laptop stays in the bag.

01Ambient Data

## Ambient Data — The Evolution of the Agentic Scribe

- **Say it once — it’s charted forever.** Every value spoken during a visit — labs, vitals, meds, allergies, or any custom metric your practice cares about — becomes coded, trended data on a chart that assembles itself. Every code is checked against current official releases before it files, so a made-up or outdated code structurally can’t get in.
- **Build custom population panels in one click.** “Every diabetic with a latest A1c above 9” — threshold panels, care-gap recall lists, and cohorts on any metric you track, across your whole panel. Per-patient trends, honest denominators, and CSV export for outreach.
- **Approve what the AI heard — with proof.** After each visit, review a short list of suggested chart updates, each carrying its verbatim quote and the exact second of audio behind it. Nothing touches the chart without your click.
- **Missed care becomes a list you can act on.** The abnormal lab that never got its retest, the diabetic with no A1c in six months, the patient told to follow up who never did — each is one question over your whole panel, exported in one click, every entry clinician-approved. Registries and care-gap worklists for clinical practices, recall and reactivation lists for cash-pay — from data nobody had to type, because the chart writes it.

04Ultra Review

## Ultra Review — Your Whole Chart, Checked Against the Literature

DeepEvidentia answered the question you asked. Ultra Review finds the ones you didn’t.

- **One button reads the entire visit.** The live transcript, the chart, every stored document summary, the timeline items you select and the chart’s newest visit note are read together — then the indexed peer-reviewed literature, clinical guidelines and FDA drug labels (DailyMed) are searched against what was actually said and charted — with a plethora of journal filters to choose from, and your own writing-style preferences.
- **Every finding filed by risk, every claim cited.** The report sorts what it finds into Critical, High, Medium, Low and Informational, with its sources inline — a finding like a requested medication that interacts with one already on the med list arrives at the top, carrying the drug label behind it.
- **Deep reasoning that runs on our servers.** Claude Fable 5.1 at maximum effort works through the evidence: about 10 minutes and 25 credits per review. Keep charting, switch patients or close the tab — the Ultra Review tab lights up when the report is ready.
- **You stay the pilot.** The review never changes the chart. Add the report to the note as a research card in one click; either way it is kept on the practice timeline. Steer the next review with a saved template as its priorities, and set evidence filters — publication year, article types and journals — right where you start it.
