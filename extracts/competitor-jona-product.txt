## Frequently Asked Questions

How is Jona different from other microbiome companies?

Jona goes beyond a narrow pathogen panel like most other tests. Unlike basic PCR methods, Jona uses deep shotgun metagenomic sequencing to identify, down to the strain level, all the bacteria, fungi, viruses, and other organisms in your patient's sample — usually leading to 10–20x more organisms detected than other tests.

What does Jona AI do?

Jona AI helps you interpret findings by identifying microbiome associations to conditions and symptoms based on 220K+ peer-reviewed studies. Using Digital Twin analysis, Jona simulates what actions your patient can take to improve their microbiome signatures — bringing the full breadth of microbiome science to your fingertips and saving you time.

How does Jona's Digital Twin work?

Jona's Digital Twin models how dietary, lifestyle, and probiotic interventions may shift the microbiome — providing insight into potential outcomes before interventions are implemented.

How can I use Jona in patient care?

Jona's customizable Action Plan allows you to set patient goals and tailor recommendations, so each patient receives a plan aligned with their clinical needs and health objectives.

How do I create a practitioner account?

Please schedule a call with our onboarding team by completing this [brief survey](https://wxjeiglz31w.typeform.com/to/cqBB40rH "https://wxjeiglz31w.typeform.com/to/cqBB40rH"). From here, our team will review your information and customize your Jona practitioner account.

Contact us at [practices@jona.health](mailto:practices@jona.health "mailto:practices@jona.health") with any questions.
