## FAQ

### What is PromicsEdge for Medical Doctors?

A precision medicine platform that integrates DNA, lab, lifestyle, symptoms, conditions, and goals to generate individualized, evidence-based care plans for your patients.

### How does the platform support precision medicine in daily practice?

It combines AI-powered analytics with clinical data to produce clear risk scores and personalized diet, supplement, and lifestyle recommendations you can use at the point of care.

### Can PromicsEdge be white-labeled for my practice?

Yes. Reports and dashboards can be delivered under your own brand so you maintain clinical authority and a seamless patient experience.

### What genetic and health reports are included?

PromicsEdge offers over 1,500 genetic reports and polygenic risk scores, including rare variants, nutrigenomics, functional health, PGx, ACMG, pathway reports, such as Methylation, Detox, Histamine, longevity risk screening, and carrier status reports for inherited conditions and more. See the full list of reports [here](https://static.selfdecode.com/misc/selfdecode-reports-features-summary.pdf).

### How is our genetic data analysis different from other services?

Most DNA testing companies analyze only a small fraction of your DNA. **PrmicsEdge analyzes up to 300× more genetic data** for deeper and more accurate insights.

- **Up to 300x More Data** – While companies like 23andMe analyze only 3,000 genetic variants (SNPs) for blood pressure, we analyze nearly **1 million**. Nebula examines just 31 SNPs by comparison.

- **200M+ Additional Variants** – Thanks to our advanced genetic imputation technology, our system transforms ~750,000 SNPs from standard genotyping chips into **over 200 million** additional data points, with an accuracy rate of 99.7%.


PromicsEdge is powered by Bayesian deep learning, hyperdimensional computing, and machine learning, the same advanced AI techniques used by leading institutions like Harvard and MIT—and even companies like Uber for driverless cars.

### Can I integrate laboratory results?

Yes. The platform supports integrating **over 4,000** laboratory biomarkers, clinical history, lifestyle factors, symptoms, reported chronic conditions and goals to generate personalized recommendations.

### What intake and patient management tools are available?

Tools include secure messaging, customizable intake forms, charting, client dashboards, meal planning, creating custome gene analysis and custon gene reporting, and the ability to attach notes and links to patient recommendations.

### How does PromicsEdge improve efficiency?

Doctors report faster treatment planning, more referrals from satisfied patients, and the ability to serve more patients without adding hours.

### Is the platform free for medical doctors?

Yes. Medical doctors can access the platform at no cost and may earn commission when patients they refer purchase kits or services.

### How does PromicsEdge differ from other solutions?

It offers broader report coverage, deeper variant analysis, extensive lab integration, and a large recommendation database, all delivered in clinically usable formats.
