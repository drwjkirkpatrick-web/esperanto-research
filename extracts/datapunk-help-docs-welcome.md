|  | ![](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/docs/help.png)<br>#### Help Docs<br>### Welcome<br>* * *<br>Welcome to **Opus 23 Pro**, a program written by Dr. [Peter D’Adamo](http://generativemedicine.org/portal/?page_id=26) for use at the [Center of Excellence in Generative Medicine](http://www.generativemedicine.org/) (COEGM) to analyze and interpret raw genomic data for use with patients at the University of Bridgeport, CT. It is curated by various editors and students at the COEGM. Opus 23 Pro is now available to physicians for use with their own patients after purchasing a user license.<br>Opus 23 Pro is a suite of apps that provide a development environment for precision medicine. It allows the clinician to import raw genomic data (such as provided by 23andMe and other services) and perform a series of extraordinarily sophisticated analytics upon it. The process culminates in a complete chart report and a fully annotated client report, which can be easily modified to display only the data the client needs to know. Opus 23 Pro features:<br>- Complex multi-SNP algorithms: Very few single SNPs have actionable consequences. The Opus 23 Pro app [LUMEN](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/analytics/lumen/index.pl) runs over 300 algorithms on multi-SNP clusters, identifying significant haplotypes and epistatic relationships<br>- Smart Interactive Network Maps: We've all studied pathway maps. However the Opus 23 Pro app [MAPPER](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/docs/index.pl?DYNAMICMAPS.pl) can populate network maps with the actual genotype data from your client. It can even try to approximate gene function based on SNP outcomes and overall network efficiency<br>- Mega-Data Mashups: Opus 23 Pro synthesizes its results from a wide variety of data, including GWAS (genome wide association study) data, dbSNP, Interactome, Etiome, PubMed, HapMap, and many others. [SUPERMOGADON](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/analytics/supermogadon/index.pl) takes GWAS data and displays it as interactive Manhattan plots. [GWASFED](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/analytics/supermogadon/gwasfed.pl) depicts Genome-Wide Association study (GWAS) data as applied to the client genotype, and links to the relevant PubMed references<br>- Causes and Effects: Opus 23 Pro uses advanced data analysis tools to surf various networks and identify high value targets. [ACCELERANT](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/analytics/etiome) identifies complicating etiologic factors for the client by combining searches of the Diseasome and Etiome<br>  <br>- Intelligent Prescriptives: Opus 23 Pro features a unique curated database of natural products known to regulate gene expression. The [PSYCHIC](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/analytics/psychic/index.pl) app surfs the Interactome and identifies upstream and downstream targets, then identifies high value agents to consider<br>- Complex Syndrome Modeling: Most complex syndromes are the result of genes acting in 'subgraphs' --specific neighborhoods in the network. [MOBOCASTER](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/analytics/mobocaster) supplies you with this data.<br>- Fully Interactive Learning Tool: It's inevitable that you'll encounter genes or SNPs you've never heard of. [Pop-ups](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/docs/index.pl?POPUPS) give you details: Click on a gene or SNP and up pops an information screen with curated gene or SNP data, where available. Click on an agent and up comes the PubMed citation<br>- Pharmacogenomics simplified: Adverse drug reactions are a potential threat to patient health. The [DRUGGIE](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/analytics/druggie/index.pl) app will report over 350 potential adverse reactions based on your client's genotype<br>. - Two smart search facilities: [WANDERER](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/analytics/wanderer/index.pl) searches the output of Opus 23 apps and algorithms for genes, SNPs and keywords; [ARGONAUT](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/analytics/navigator/index.pl) searches the client's raw data for genes, SNPs and curated SNP data text, as well as specific genotypes<br>- Two smart search facilities: [WANDERER](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/analytics/supermogadon/gwasfed.pl) searches the output of Opus 23 apps and algorithms for genes, SNPs and keywords; [GWASFED](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/analytics/supermogadon/gwasfed.pl) ARGONAUT searches the client's raw data for genes, SNPs and curated SNP data text, as well as specific genotypes<br>- Safe and Secure: Opus 23 Pro uses SSL (https) to protect sensitive data during transmission. In addition all data on the Opus 23 server is protected with AEM (Authenticated Encryption Methodologies) encryption that actually exceeds HIPAA standards<br>- And Much More: The Opus 23 Pro app [RARIFY](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/analytics/rarify) checks for rare alleles. The [FANTASMATRON](https://www.datapunk.net/opus23/analytics/fantasmatron/index.pl) app allows you to make dietary suggestions based on client genotype<br>These Help Docs will take you through all these aspects of Opus 23 Pro. |
