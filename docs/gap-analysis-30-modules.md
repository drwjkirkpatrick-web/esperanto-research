OPUS23 | ESPERANTO Gap Analysis 30 Candidate Modules Derivable from Existing Data Assets
Prepared for: Dr. Peter D'Adamo | Platform: OPUS23 | ESPERANTO (Datapunk Bioinformatics)
Executive Summary
OPUS23 | ESPERANTO represents a generational leap in multi-omics clinical decision support, integrating 4,500+ curated SNPs, 22,800 microbial taxa, and 3,200+ metabolites across three instruments (COGENT, FLORIAN, KINESIS) with a unified reasoning layer powered by a dedicated large language model.
This analysis identifies 30 modules that can be constructed primarily from the information assets already resident in the ESPERANTO vault—without requiring new assay integrations, wet-lab partnerships, or novel data streams.
Competitive Landscape Context
ESPERANTO's moat is the unification of genome + microbiome + metabolome in one client record with reasoning. Competitors compared:
Jona — Deep shotgun metagenomics, Digital Twin simulations. Weakness: No native genomic integration; SNP data external.
PromicsEdge — White-label DNA reports, ancestry-informed PRS. Weakness: No metabolome instrument; gut data limited.
Tiny Health PRO — Strain-level gut + vaginal microbiome. Weakness: No genetic or metabolomic reasoning layer.
GX Sciences — Turn-key nutrigenomic panels. Weakness: Small SNP panel (~120 genes); no microbiome.
DeepCura — IFM Matrix + AI scribe, DUTCH/GI-MAP integration. Weakness: Generic LLM; not grounded to curated SNP database.
Metabolon IBP — Enterprise multi-omics (metagenome + metabolome). Weakness: Research-oriented; no clinical decision support.
3MCor / MMIP — Academic microbiome-metabolome correlation. Weakness: No genome; no practitioner-facing reports.
FunctionalDX — Functional blood chemistry interpretation. Weakness: No genetics or microbiome.
Methodology
Each module evaluated against four criteria:
Data Availability — Built from existing SNPs, taxa, metabolites already in ESPERANTO.
Clinical Utility — Addresses a real practitioner decision point.
Cross-Instrument Synergy — Requires 2-3 of COGENT / FLORIAN / KINESIS.
Implementation Complexity — Estimated engineering effort.
The 30 Modules
Module 1 — Pharmacogenomic Dosing Calculator (PGx Core) Converts CYP2D6, CYP2C19, CYP3A4/5, CYP2C9, TPMT, DPYD, SLCO1B1, and VKORC1 genotypes into FDA/CPIC-guided prescribing recommendations with drug-by-drug dosing adjustments. Data: Existing SNP panel + drug-gene interactions in COGENT; ClinVar PGx annotations. Cross-Instrument: COGENT -> KINESIS | Complexity: Low
Module 2 — Methylation Capacity Index (MCI) Composite score aggregating MTHFR (C677T, A1298C), MTR, MTRR, BHMT, PEMT, COMT, MAT1A, and SHMT1 variants into a single "methylation flux" metric with bottleneck readouts. Data: 4,500+ SNP panel covers all major methylation genes. Cross-Instrument: COGENT + KINESIS | Complexity: Low
Module 3 — Detoxification Burden Score (DBS) Quantifies phase I (CYP1A2, CYP2E1) vs. phase II (GSTT1, GSTM1, GSTP1, SULT1A1, NAT2, UGT1A1) capacity relative to toxic exposure markers to estimate whether detox architecture matches measured load. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS organic acids / toxic metabolites. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Low-Medium
Module 4 — Mitochondrial Haplogroup Stress Index (MHSI) From WGS VCF data, assigns mitochondrial haplogroup and correlates with known associations. Cross-references with Mitome cheek-swab data if present. Data: mtDNA variants + Mitome import already supported. Cross-Instrument: COGENT + KINESIS | Complexity: Medium
Module 5 — ABO-Secretor-Disease Risk Matrix (ASDRM) Leverages ABO, FUT2 (secretor), and Lewis status to generate conditional risk profiles: non-secretor + Crohn's, A + cardiovascular, O + norovirus resistance, AB + cognitive decline. Provides food and supplement recommendations. Data: COGENT already reports ABO, FUT2, Lewis.
Cross-Instrument: COGENT + FLORIAN | Complexity: Low
Module 6 — Histamine Intolerance Score (HIS) Combines DAO (AOC1), HNMT, MAOA, MAOB, ALDH2 genotypes with gut bacteria known to produce histamine or degrade it, producing a "histamine load vs. clearance" ratio. Data: COGENT SNPs + FLORIAN 22,800-taxon database. Cross-Instrument: COGENT + FLORIAN + KINESIS | Complexity: Medium
Module 7 — Oxalate Metabolism Risk Score (OMRS) Scores AGXT, GRHPR, HOGA1, SLC26A1 variants against Oxalobacter formigenes and other oxalate-degrading taxa in FLORIAN. Flags calcium oxalate kidney stone risk. Data: COGENT SNPs + FLORIAN taxonomic presence/absence. Cross-Instrument: COGENT + FLORIAN + KINESIS | Complexity: Low
Module 8 — Lactose Persistence <-> Gut Lactobacillus Correlator (LP-GLC) Matches LCT -13910 C/T genotype against abundance of Lactobacillus spp. and Bifidobacteria in FLORIAN. Data: COGENT LCT SNP + FLORIAN taxonomic abundance. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> FLORIAN | Complexity: Low
Module 9 — Gluten Sensitivity Polygenic Panel (GSPP) Beyond HLA-DQ2/DQ8, adds non-HLA SNPs (MYO9B, IL21) and cross-references with zonulin, calprotectin, anti-gliadin markers in KINESIS to stratify risk. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS GI-MAP/zonulin. Cross-Instrument: COGENT + FLORIAN + KINESIS | Complexity: Medium
Module 10 — Sulfur Metabolism Index (SMI) Scores CBS, SUOX, BHMT, CTH variants against sulfate-reducing bacteria (Desulfovibrio) in FLORIAN and urinary sulfate/sulfite in KINESIS. Data: COGENT SNPs + FLORIAN taxa + KINESIS sulfur markers. Cross-Instrument: All three instruments | Complexity: Medium
Module 11 — Vitamin D Metabolism Score (VDMS) Integrates CYP2R1, GC (VDBP), VDR (FokI, BsmI, ApaI, TaqI), CYP24A1 genotypes with 25-OH-D and 1,25-OH-D values to estimate conversion efficiency, transport capacity, receptor sensitivity, and degradation rate. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS vitamin D metabolites. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Low
Module 12 — Iron Metabolism Index (IMI) Combines HFE (C282Y, H63D), TMPRSS6, TFR2, SLC40A1 variants with ferritin, TIBC, serum iron, transferrin saturation to distinguish genetic hemochromatosis risk from functional iron deficiency. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS blood labs. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Low
Module 13 — Cholesterol Synthesis vs. Absorption Balance (CSAB) Compares HMGCR (synthesis) and NPC1L1 (absorption) genotypes with APOE phenotype to predict whether statins or ezetimibe would be preferred, cross-referenced with lipid panels. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS Cardio Check / routine lipids. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Low-Medium
Module 14 — Estrogen Metabolism Safety Index (EMSI) Expands existing CYP1B1/CYP1A1 integration adding COMT, SULT1A1, GSTP1, UGT1A1 variants to DUTCH estrogen metabolite panel, calculating a 4-OH:2-OH:16alpha-OH pathway safety ratio weighted by genotype-driven enzyme efficiency. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS DUTCH estrogen metabolites. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Medium
Module 15 — Cortisol Clearance Genotype-Phenotype Mismatch (CCGM) Compares CYP3A4/5, HSD11B1, HSD11B2 genotypes against DUTCH/ASI cortisol curves. Explains cases where total cortisol is normal but free cortisol is aberrant—or vice versa. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS DUTCH/ASI cortisol data. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Medium
Module 16 — Thyroid Conversion Efficiency (TCE) Models DIO1, DIO2 (Thr92Ala), DIO3, THRB variants against TSH, free T4, free T3, reverse T3 to flag "conversion hypothyroidism" vs. primary vs. receptor resistance. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS thyroid panel. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Low
Module 17 — Neurotransmitter Synthesis Capacity (NSC) Aggregates TPH2, DDC, MAOA, MAOB, COMT, DBH, GAD1/2 variants with organic acids (5-HIAA, HVA, VMA) and neurotransmitter metabolites to estimate synthesis vs. degradation balance. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS organic acids/neurotransmitter markers. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Medium
Module 18 — One-Carbon Flux Model (OCFM) Dynamic systems model of methionine-folate-B12 cycle using MTHFR, MTR, MTRR, BHMT, RFC1, TCN2, CBS, MUT SNPs, fed by homocysteine, FIGLU, MMA from KINESIS. Outputs bottleneck identification. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS amino acid / OAT markers. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: High
Module 19 — Omega-3 Responsive Index Prediction (ORIP) Predicts Omega-3 Index response to EPA/DHA supplementation using FADS1 (rs174537), FADS2, ELOVL2 genotypes, then validates against actual Omega-3 Index results. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS Omega-3 Index. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Low
Module 20 — Gut-Brain Axis Risk Score (GBARS) Cross-references FLORIAN taxa producing GABA, serotonin precursors, butyrate, LPS with COGENT neurotransmitter SNPs. Flags "double-hit" patients with genetic vulnerability + microbial dysbiosis. Data: FLORIAN taxa annotations + COGENT SNPs. Cross-Instrument: COGENT + FLORIAN | Complexity: Medium
Module 21 — TMAO Cardiovascular Risk Integrator (TCRI) Scores FMO3 genotype against choline/carnitine-rich diet and TMA-producing bacteria (Proteus, Providencia, Desulfovibrio) in FLORIAN. Predicts TMAO-mediated cardiovascular risk without direct TMAO measurement. Data: COGENT FMO3 + FLORIAN taxa + KINESIS choline metabolites. Cross-Instrument: All three instruments | Complexity: Medium
Module 22 — Urea Cycle Capacity Profiler (UCCP) Models CPS1, ASS1, ASL, ARG1, OTC variants against ammonia, amino acid panel, and organic acids to flag low-function urea cycle states. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS amino acids / ammonia. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Medium
Module 23 — Glycemic Response Prediction (GRP) Combines TCF7L2, SLC30A8, PPARG, KCNJ11, FTO variants with fasting insulin, glucose, HbA1c, HOMA-IR to predict carbohydrate sensitivity and post-prandial glycemic excursions. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS cardiometabolic labs. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Low-Medium
Module 24 — Saturated Fat Sensitivity Index (SFSI) Uses APOA2 (rs5082) and PPARG variants to predict whether saturated fat intake raises LDL disproportionately, cross-checked with lipid panels. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS lipid panel. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Low
Module 25 — Caffeine Metabolism Index (CMI) CYP1A2 genotype predicts caffeine half-life; cross-referenced with cortisol awakening response (CAR) from DUTCH/ASI and sleep markers to flag "slow metabolizer + HPA dysregulation" where caffeine is contraindicated. Data: COGENT CYP1A2 + KINESIS DUTCH/ASI cortisol curves. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Low
Module 26 — Alcohol Metabolism Risk Profile (AMRP) ADH1B, ADH1C, ALDH2 genotypes predict acetaldehyde exposure; cross-referenced with GGT, ALT, AST, MCV to stratify liver risk and recommend abstinence thresholds. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS liver enzymes. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Low
Module 27 — Salicylate / Phase II Sensitivity Score (SPII) NAT2 (slow/fast acetylator), UGT1A1, SULT1A1 variants predict sensitivity to salicylates, benzoates, phenolic compounds. Cross-referenced with food antibody panels and organic acids. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS food antibodies / OAT. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Low
Module 28 — Gut Mucosal Integrity Index (GMII) Combines tight-junction SNPs (CDH1, OCLN, TJP1) with FLORIAN zonulin producers (Akkermansia muciniphila, Faecalibacterium prausnitzii) and KINESIS zonulin, calprotectin, IgA to produce a single mucosal health score. Data: COGENT SNPs + FLORIAN taxa + KINESIS GI markers. Cross-Instrument: All three instruments | Complexity: Medium
Module 29 — Bone Remodeling Balance (BRB) Integrates VDR (BsmI, FokI), COL1A1 (Sp1), ESR1, GC variants with bone turnover markers and vitamin D/calcium metabolism to predict osteoporosis risk trajectory. Data: COGENT SNPs + KINESIS calcium, PTH, vitamin D. Cross-Instrument: COGENT <-> KINESIS | Complexity: Low-Medium
Module 30 — Inflammatory Burden Polygenic Score (IBPS) Aggregates IL6 (rs1800795), TNF (rs1800629), IL1B, CRP (rs1205), NFKB1 variants into a polygenic inflammation propensity score, modulated by FLORIAN endotoxin-producers and KINESIS hs-CRP. Data: COGENT SNPs + FLORIAN taxa + KINESIS inflammatory markers. Cross-Instrument: All three instruments | Complexity: Medium
Implementation Roadmap

Phase 2 (Cross-Instrument) — 8 to 10 weeks Modules: 3, 6, 7, 9, 10, 13, 14, 15, 17, 21, 24, 27 Rationale: Requires taxa annotation or metabolite ratio modeling.
Phase 3 (Systems Modeling) — 12 to 16 weeks Modules: 4, 18, 20, 22, 23, 28, 29, 30 Rationale: Constraint-based or multi-parameter scoring; highest clinical value.
Closing Note
None of these modules require ESPERANTO to sequence new analytes, sign new lab contracts, or ingest novel file formats. They are derivatives of the existing vault—curated SNPs, taxa, metabolites, and the pathway knowledge that already connects them. What changes is the presentation: moving from isolated variant reports to composite clinical indices that answer the practitioner's real question: "Given everything I know about this patient, what is the most likely physiological bottleneck, and what should I address first?"
The competitors can match file parsers and LLM wrappers. They cannot easily replicate a decade of curated biochemical-individuality logic. These 30 modules make that logic computable, patient-specific, and actionable.
